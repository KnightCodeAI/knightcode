# Getting started

A bot is an assistant with its own chat, memory, files and computer. Create one
from the sidebar's New Bot button, start from blank or a template, and give it a
name and instructions.

- Your bots run on your own AI provider accounts. Connect them in Settings >
  General.
- Talk to a bot in its chat. It keeps working when you close the app; you get a
  notification when it needs you.
- Connect apps (mail, calendar, code, files) from Connect apps so a bot can use
  them.
- Choose how bots ask before acting in Settings > Approvals.

## Group chats

Choose New group chat in Search, or New group with a bot from its sidebar menu.
The chat column opens a To: field. Select bots from the suggestions, or type
to search by name. Each selected bot appears as a chip; click its × to remove
it, or press Backspace in an empty field to remove the last bot. After selecting
2 to 10 bots, press Enter in the empty field to create and open the group.
The first selected bot becomes the lead. The initial name lists the bots in
selection order: “New and Finley” or “New, Finley, and Random.”
Press Escape to cancel.

Click a group's name above its conversation to open or close its details pane.
The pane has Details, Library and Members tabs. Library shows files shared in
recent messages. Edit the group name above the tabs. Details contains the lead
and turn budget. Changes save immediately; there is no separate group-settings
dialog.

In Members, click a bot to open its chat. Hover over a member to reveal Remove,
or choose Add Member and search for another bot. Changes save immediately.
Groups need 2 to 10 bots. If you remove the lead, the first remaining member
becomes the lead.
