# Payments

In the US, a bot can buy things for you from the wallet you link in Settings,
Payments.

- Each bot has a limit per purchase and per month; a purchase over the limit is
  refused.
- Every purchase asks first and is approved with your passkey, over that exact
  merchant, item and amount.
- The bot pays with a single-use card or token limited to that purchase. Card
  numbers never appear in chat, logs or screenshots.
