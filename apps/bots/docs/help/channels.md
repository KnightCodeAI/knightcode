# Chat apps and email

Every bot has its own email address on bots.knightcode.dev and answers mail in
the same thread. You can also talk to a bot from Telegram and Slack; the
conversation is the same thread you see in the web and desktop apps.

- Telegram: in Settings, Chat apps, pick a bot and send the code shown to the
  KnightCode Bots Telegram bot.
- Slack: Add to Slack installs the app in your workspace; Link DMs sends your
  direct messages with it to one bot.
- Mail and messages from other people are outside text: they can steer a bot,
  but never widen what it may do.
